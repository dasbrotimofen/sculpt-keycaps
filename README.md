# Sculpt-keycaps
Parametric keycap generator based on "CadQuery"

<img width="2403" height="940" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6df8f671-9351-453d-b1c0-c8a1a90d1dd9" />



Use "Sculpt" and CQ‑editor to adjust the parameters for the most versatile, flexible, and simple keycap creation. Now you can turn all your ideas into reality and take your keyboard’s comfort to a whole new level.  
Just enter the desired values and get a ready‑to‑print model.  

The code generates not only the keycaps themselves, but also easily removable supports that do not damage the appearance of the finished parts. You can place the models directly on the 3D‑printer bed at the optimal angle for printing, ensuring maximum quality.
